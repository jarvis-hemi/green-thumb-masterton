Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If yours isn't listed, just give us a call.

How quickly can you start?

Most new clients are scheduled within 3–5 business days. For emergency clean-ups, we can usually be out to your property the next morning.

Do you provide free quotes?

Absolutely. Routine lawn mowing quotes are given over the phone in under two minutes. For custom garden work, we visit your property free of charge and provide a written quote with no obligation.

What if it rains on my scheduled day?

If conditions are unsafe (heavy rain, flooding), we'll reschedule you for the next available slot at no extra charge. We'll also leave a note on your door so you know why.

Can I cancel my service anytime?

Yes — absolutely. We operate on a month-to-month basis with no lock-in contracts. Just give us a text or call 48 hours before your next service, and we won't come back.

Do you use pesticides or herbicides?

We prefer organic and natural solutions wherever practical. We can discuss specific chemical treatments on a case-by-case basis, especially for established weed problems — but our default is always the safest option for families, pets, and gardens.

Are you insured?

Yes — we hold full public liability insurance ($5 million cover) so your property is always protected. Certificates of currency are available on request.
